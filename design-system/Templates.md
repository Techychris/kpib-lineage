# Templates

Brand pieces made outside the website: social posts, print and email. Each is a component with a live preview; these rules apply to all of them.

## Shared rules

- **Artboards are fixed.** Templates do not follow the light or dark theme: grounds are `brand-black` or white, text is white or black, red appears only as the stripes.
- **One message per piece.** A title of four words or fewer, uppercase at weight 800, then when, where and who. No paragraphs.
- **The mark.** The crest on a white `logo-plate` for social; the primary lockup for print. Never on a photo without the plate.
- **Photos.** Real chapter photos only, under a `scrim` where text sits on them. A chapter with no photos uses the type layout instead of another chapter's pictures.
- **Facts.** Dates, places and names are confirmed by the chapter before posting. Previews use samples, labeled as such.
- **Margins.** About 7% of the short side (72 px on a 1080 post, 0.5 in on a letter flyer).

## Sizes and export

| Piece | Size | Export |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram post | 1080 by 1080 px | PNG |
| Instagram story | 1080 by 1920 px; keep text out of the top 250 and bottom 340 px | PNG |
| Event flyer | 8.5 by 11 in, 0.125 in bleed if printed edge to edge | PDF |
| Name tag | 4 by 3 in | PDF, on adhesive or insert stock |
| Email signature | 14px Arial, literal hex values | HTML pasted into the mail client |

## Captions and posts

Write like the website: first-person plural, short sentences, no emoji strings, no hype ("Join us Thursday at 7:00 PM in the chapter room", not "Don't miss this!!! 🔥"). Tag the chapter account and use the full name, Kappa Pi Beta, at least once per post. Hashtags: the chapter handle and at most two others.

## Who signs off

The chapter President or Rush Chair confirms facts; anything that uses national's name in print or a new design (merchandise, banners) goes to the national board first. Print color follows the open red decision in **Decisions**.
